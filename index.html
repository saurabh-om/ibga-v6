For Parents

Studying More Doesn’t Always Mean Scoring More.

Many intelligent students put in significant effort, but still don’t get the results they expect. The challenge is often in understanding concepts deeply, applying them correctly and writing answers the way they are assessed.

At IBGA, we bridge this gap with the right teaching, practice and feedback.

How we mark practice papers

Practice papers, marked the way an examiner would.

When your child sits a practice paper with us, it comes back within 72 hours with a note beside every mark they lost, explaining what to change.

  • Every mark reviewed
  • Clear, practical feedback
  • Helps them improve faster

IB Diploma · Economics · Paper 1Question 3(b)

Evaluate the view that a minimum wage is the best way to reduce poverty. [15]

  1. A minimum wage is a price floor set above the equilibrium wage.

    Good start.
    Clear definition.

  2. It lifts the income of low-paid workers who keep their jobs.

    Right. That’s knowledge, and it’s earning marks.

  3. Set too high, firms hire less labour and unemployment rises.

    Good evaluation. Consider real-world example here.

  4. On balance, it helps only when set near the market wage and paired with transfers.

    Yes. Strong conclusion. You could add a specific example.

Mark/ 15

Next time: include a real-world example to strengthen your evaluation.

Quick answers to common questions about our programmes, classes and support.

Your
Questions.
Our Support.

Which programmes do you offer?

We offer IB MYP (Grades 6–10), IB DP (Grades 11–12) and IGCSE (Grades 9–10) across key subjects.

How do you assess my child’s progress?

We use topic-wise assignments, regular tests and detailed feedback to track learning.

Do you offer online and offline classes?

Yes. We offer both online classes and offline classes at our four Gurugram centres.

Are classes one-to-one or in small groups?

We offer both 1-to-1 and small group classes, depending on your child’s needs and goals.

What are the fees and how are classes booked?

Our fees depend on the subject, programme and class format. Please get in touch for details.

When can my child start?

You can start anytime. We usually begin with a discussion and a demo class to understand your child’s needs.
